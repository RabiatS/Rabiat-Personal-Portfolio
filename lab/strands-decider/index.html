Rabiat's LabStrands Decider 2B

A model that only decides

Strands Decider does not write. It reads a situation and one typed question, then scores every option in a single pass. The bars are its probabilities.

Pick one chooses between options. Scale rates on levels, lowest first. Yes or no checks whether a statement holds, and works best with a plain statement rather than a question.

This is the 4-bit build (q4f16). Next to the full model it can flip near-ties, so treat a close split as a coin toss. It was made for routing and triage inside software, so take its life advice lightly.

Runs on your device. Nothing you type is uploaded.
