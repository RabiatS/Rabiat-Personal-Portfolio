Forecasting, untrained

Chronos-2 reads up to the last 512 values of a series and says what probably comes next: a middle line, and a band where 8 in 10 outcomes should land (the 10th to 90th percentile).

I once hand-built a stock trend predictor out of moving averages and linear regression, tuned for that one stock. This model needs no training on your data at all; it has learned what series in general tend to do.

Steps, café and power are made up on this page. The CO₂ record is NOAA's monthly mean at Mauna Loa Observatory, 1985 to 2026 (gml.noaa.gov, public domain). Drop in a CSV to try your own.

Runs on your device. Your data is never uploaded.
