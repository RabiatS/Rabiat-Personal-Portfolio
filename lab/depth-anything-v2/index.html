Depth from one photo

The model looks at a single photo and guesses how far away every pixel is. Here each pixel is pushed forward or back by that guess, so the photo becomes a scene you can tilt.

Move over it, drag it, or tilt your phone. The Depth button shows what the model saw: brighter is nearer.
