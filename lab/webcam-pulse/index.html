Your pulse, from your webcam

Each heartbeat pushes a little more blood into your face, and your skin's colour shifts by far less than you could see. ME-rPPG looks at a small crop of your face 30 times a second and turns those shifts into a pulse wave. The number is the strongest rhythm in the last ten seconds.

In 2024 I built a heart disease predictor, Pulse Predictors, from tabular data. This one reads the pulse from tiny colour changes in your skin instead.

The portrait is my illustration with a pulse painted in at the rate it shows, so you can see it work without a camera.

A fun demo, not a medical device. Don't use it to check your health. Nothing is recorded or uploaded: the video stays on your device.

The face finder is BlazeFace on MediaPipe, both Apache 2.0, from Google. The steps around the model follow the official ME-rPPG web demo (Apache 2.0).
