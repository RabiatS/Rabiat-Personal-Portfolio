The Lab

When a new openly licensed model comes out and I want to see what it can do, it gets a page here, built around the thing it does best.

Every model runs on your device, in your browser. Before anything downloads, each page says where it will run and how big it is, and it won't download a model your device can't handle. After the first visit it stays cached.

Open licences only. Each page's (i) has the size, speed on your device, licence and source.
