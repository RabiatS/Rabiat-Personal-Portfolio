Lab

New models come out every day. This is where I try them before building them into anything: what they can and can't do, and how they run on your own device. Nothing to install, nothing uploaded.

By the month each model came out, newest first. The ones that run here say how big they are and where they will run before anything downloads, and stay on your device after the first visit. The ones too big for a browser link to a demo instead.
