Rabiat'sPDF Merger

How it works

Everything happens in your browser. Your PDFs are never uploaded, so contracts and IDs stay on your device.

  • Drag the cards to set the order
  • Click All pages on a card to pick pages, like 1-3, 5 or 4-
  • Drop more files anywhere to add them

Built with pdf-lib and pdf.js. Password-protected PDFs need unlocking first.
