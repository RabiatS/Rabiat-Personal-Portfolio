How it works

The model runs on your device, on your GPU when it can. Your image is never uploaded.

  • ⌘V paste an image, any time
  • ⌘C copy the result, ready to paste anywhere
  • Drag the result straight out of the page
  • Hold Space to peek at the original

Full resolution, no watermark.

The first image downloads the model once (84 MB, or 42 MB without a GPU). After that it starts instantly. Model: ORMBG, Apache 2.0.
