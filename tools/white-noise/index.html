How it works

Every sound is generated live in your browser. There are no recordings, so there is no loop you can learn to hear, and nothing to download.

  • Tap a sound to add it, use its slider to set how much
  • Space plays and pauses
  • The timer fades out gently instead of cutting off
  • Your mix is remembered on this device

White is bright and even. Pink is softer, like steady rain. Brown is deep, like a far-off waterfall. Many people find brown or pink easiest to focus with.
