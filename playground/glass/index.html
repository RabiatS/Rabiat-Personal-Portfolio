Glass

This is the glass from my site's header and nav, on its own. Slide from clear to frost and drag the pane over the colour.

The edge works like a real lens: it bends what is behind it and splits the colours a little. As it frosts, the blur comes up and the bend eases off.

The bend needs Chrome or Edge. Safari and Firefox can't bend a live backdrop yet, so they get the frost and the lit rim. Lens maths ported from Glisse by Kelvin Zhang (MIT).
