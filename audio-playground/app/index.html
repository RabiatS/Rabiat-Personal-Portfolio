01Edit

Frequency bands

  • Splits by pitch: low (bass, kick), mid (voice, chords), high (cymbals, air).
  • Instant, but it won't isolate a single instrument.
  • The numbers set where one band ends and the next begins.

03Stems

  • ▶ plays every stem together.
  • S solo: hear only that stem. M mute: silence it.
  • Click a waveform to jump there. Edit opens a stem in the editor.
  • Download mix saves exactly what you're hearing.
