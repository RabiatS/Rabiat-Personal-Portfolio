What it does

Three things, done well

01

Cut & join

Drag across a waveform to select seconds 5 to 7, delete them, and the rest joins up seamlessly. Undo anything.

02

Split into stems

A machine-learning model separates the song into its parts. Solo, mute and remix them.

VocalsDrums BassGuitar PianoOther
03

Download anything

Each stem as WAV or MP3, all stems in one ZIP, or just the mix you're hearing (like a karaoke version).

How it runs

Your device does the work

Pick a model

The first time, your browser downloads the model (60–91 MB) and keeps it. After that it loads instantly.

Drop a song

The file is read inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server; there is no server doing the splitting at all.

Split, listen, download

Your graphics chip runs the model (about 3–6× faster than real time on a recent laptop). Then mix and export.

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes. The processing happens on your computer, so there's nothing to pay for.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. Songs are opened and processed inside your browser tab. Only the model file is downloaded, once.

How good is the separation?

Vocals, drums and bass come out very clean. Guitar and piano are harder and may carry a little of other instruments. Busy, heavily produced songs are the hardest.

Can I use the stems in my own music?

Splitting songs for practice, study or fun is fine. Publishing stems taken from someone else's song still needs their permission.
