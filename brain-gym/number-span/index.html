Number Span

Digits appear one at a time, about one a second. When they stop, type them back in order.

Start at 3. Each right answer adds a digit. You get two tries at each length, and the run ends when both miss.

Working memory, the space you hold things in while you think. George A. Miller, "The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two", Psychological Review 63(2), 1956.
